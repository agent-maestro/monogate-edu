# Hardware Requirements

Hardware is course-specific. Do not buy or wire parts until the course bill of
materials says they are needed.

For the first course, the physical path is an ESP32 breadboard build with a
potentiometer, LED, tactile button, passive piezo buzzer, and resistors.

General bench expectations:

- Low-voltage USB-powered work only.
- Breadboard jumpers and a stable work surface.
- One change at a time.
- Disconnect USB before changing wiring.
- Stop if anything heats, smells wrong, resets repeatedly, or behaves in a way
  the course does not describe.

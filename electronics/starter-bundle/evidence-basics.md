# Evidence Basics

Monogate Electronics courses do not stop at "it worked once."

Each finished lab should leave behind:

- the kernel or rule being tested;
- the trace path;
- the validator result;
- the replay result;
- hardware notes or simulation notes;
- claim flags that say what was and was not observed.

Simulation is useful evidence, but it is not physical observation. Physical
claims require real hardware capture and review.
